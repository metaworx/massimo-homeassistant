# NOTE Integration v1.2: the icon

The owner's decision of 2026-11-13, for Block 6 (first release) and for
Massimo's own icon.

## Decision D13-Z4

One mark for Massimo and the integration, which the owner, as the licence
holder of both, allows the integration to carry under its Apache-2.0 licence:

- a bold M with round corners, in Massimo's blue (`#1F63C6`), on a transparent
  background; a lighter blue (`#5B95EC`) on dark backgrounds;
- the M's V at a right angle, its point the hub of a clock,
  the hands cut out along the V's arms: a short hour hand, a minute hand about
  one and a half times as long;
- a thermometer cut out of each leg, column and bulb; the left column full,
  the right one about half;
- a five-step scale on both: dots left standing in the mercury, holes cut
  above it, the right thermometer's two holes raised a little above their
  true steps for balance.

The logo puts the mark in place of the M of "Massimo", the letters on the M's
baseline, in a regular weight.

## How it was reached

From Massimo's app icon (a white house on blue), through a house with an M,
thermometer legs, the V as clock hands, to the M itself carved;
the gates of 2026-11-13 hold each round.

## What follows

- The master drawings and the exports (SVG, PNG, ICO, three animated GIFs) are
  made from one generator, outside the repository until Block 6;
  Block 6 puts `icon.png`, `icon@2x.png`, `dark_icon.png`, `dark_icon@2x.png`,
  `logo.png`, `logo@2x.png`, `dark_logo.png` and `dark_logo@2x.png` into
  `custom_components/massimo/brand/`.
- The logo's typeface is provisional (Lato Regular, as outlines) until the
  owner chooses one.
- Massimo's new icon is Massimo's change, under a plan of its own.
